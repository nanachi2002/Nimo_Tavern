Provider connection

By default your browser talks to AI providers directly, and NimoTavern never sees your prompts or keys. Some providers block requests from browsers (CORS). For those, you can turn on the NimoTavern relay.

With the relay, the request (prompt and API key) passes through this server's memory on its way to the provider. It's never logged or stored, but you have to trust the server while it's in transit. Leave it off unless you need it.

Images & storage

Pictures you add (backgrounds, expression sprites, chat images) are converted to WebP right in your browser, before they are encrypted. Same look, but PNG screenshots and artwork often shrink a lot, so your storage lasts longer. Animated images (GIF, APNG) and character cards (which must stay PNG) are never changed.

Change password
