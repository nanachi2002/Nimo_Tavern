Your tavern. Your eyes only.

NimoTavern is a free, public SillyTavern instance where every chat, character, persona and API key is encrypted in your browser before it's saved. Not even the person running this server can read them.

No email. No tracking. No ads. Just a username and a password.

End-to-end encrypted

Everything is sealed with AES-256 using a key derived from your password. The server only stores unreadable ciphertext, and even the file names are hidden.

Your API keys stay yours

Requests to OpenRouter, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek and others go straight from your browser to the provider. Your keys and prompts never pass through NimoTavern.

Fast

Your tavern runs locally in your browser, so loading characters, swiping and editing chats feels instant. The interface is cached after your first visit.

The full SillyTavern

The SillyTavern you know: character cards, group chats, World Info and lorebooks, presets, personas, themes, Quick Replies, regex, and more.

Synced across devices

Log in on your phone and your PC: your encrypted data syncs automatically. You can export everything as a standard SillyTavern backup whenever you like.

Open source

NimoTavern is a fork of SillyTavern under the AGPL-3.0. The code is public, so anyone can check that it does what it says.

How your privacy is protected

You type your password
Your browser derives your encryption key (Argon2id)
Chats & cards are encrypted locally (AES-256-GCM)
The server stores only ciphertext

Your password never leaves your device. The server receives a separate login proof that can't be turned back into your password or your key. A database leak exposes only encrypted blobs.

That means if you lose your password and your recovery phrase, nobody can get your data back, including us. You get the recovery phrase when you sign up, so keep it safe. Read the full details and limitations.

Built on SillyTavern

NimoTavern exists thanks to SillyTavern, the LLM frontend for power users built by Cohee, RossAscends, Wolfsblvt and hundreds of contributors. All credit for the interface and features goes to them. NimoTavern adds accounts, end-to-end encryption, PostgreSQL storage and in-browser execution so a public instance can be run without seeing anyone's data.

If you can run SillyTavern on your own computer, that's still the most private option. NimoTavern is for when you can't, or want your tavern everywhere.
