Getting started → Connect an AI → Add a character → Chat. That's 10 minutes, and you'll have a working tavern.

NimoTavern is built by the Nimo team on top of SillyTavern, the most complete roleplay and chat frontend for AI models (created by the SillyTavern team and its contributors), and runs it as a private, encrypted, multi-user website. It has a lot of buttons. This wiki explains them in plain language: what each feature does, when you'd use it, and the exact clicks to get there.

How to read this wiki

  • Animated demos show the real interface being used. Press play, pause, or step through with the arrows.
  • Interactive widgets let you play with the ideas (sampling, context limits, lorebooks, encryption) right in the page.
  • Press / anywhere to search. Inside the tavern, the Wiki button next to Account in User Settings brings you here.
  • Look for the colored boxes: tip warning and the blue NimoTavern notes about what's different here.
Curious about the privacy part?

Start with How NimoTavern keeps you private, then verify it yourself. You don't have to trust us: the wiki shows you how to check.
