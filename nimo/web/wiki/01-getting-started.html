What is NimoTavern?

SillyTavern is an interface for talking to AI language models. It doesn't contain an AI itself: it connects to one (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, or a model running on your own PC) and adds everything around it: characters, memory, lorebooks, presets, group chats, themes and much more.

NimoTavern is the same SillyTavern, hosted for you, with one big difference: everything you save is encrypted in your browser first. The server only ever holds unreadable data. You don't have to install anything, and your chats follow you to every device.

Setup in five steps

  1. Create your account Go to the sign-up page, pick a username (3–32 characters: letters, numbers, _ . -) and a strong password of at least 10 characters. No email needed.
  2. Save your recovery phrase You'll see 12 words. They are the only way to get back in if you forget your password, because nobody (not even the admin) can reset it. Download or copy them somewhere safe, like a password manager, then tick the box.
  3. Connect an AI Click the plug icon at the top, choose a provider, paste your API key. Full guide here.
  4. Pick or import a character Open the character panel (right-most icon). Seraphina is included. Import your own cards with the button. Characters guide.
  5. Say hello Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter. Watch the answer stream in. Chatting guide.
Opened the link inside Discord, Instagram or another app?

Those built-in browsers can't run NimoTavern (especially on iPhone). Tap ••• or the share icon and choose Open in Safari (or Chrome) first. Private/incognito tabs can block it too.

Where files liveA folder on your PCEncrypted in your browser, synced as ciphertext

Who can read your chatsWhoever has the PCOnly you (with your password / recovery phrase)
