SillyTavern hides almost everything behind the icons of the top bar. Once you know the nine of them, you know where everything is. Click an icon in the mock-up below to see what it does.

The bottom bar

Under the chat you'll find the message box and a few controls:

  • Options menu (left): regenerate, continue, impersonate, start a new chat, manage chat files, attach files, close the chat.
  • Extensions menu (the magic wand, if your version shows it): quick access to extension actions such as image generation.
  • Send: sends the message. It turns into a Stop button while the AI is writing.
