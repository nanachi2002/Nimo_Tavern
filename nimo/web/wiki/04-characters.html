A character card is the AI's script: who it is, how it talks, what the scene is, and how the story starts. The model itself is the same for every character; the card is what makes Seraphina feel different from a pirate or a tutor.

Importing a character

Cards are shared as PNG images (the character data is hidden inside the picture) or JSON files. SillyTavern also reads newer formats like .charx.

  1. Download a card from a community site or a friend. A PNG that looks like a normal picture is fine, don't convert or recompress it.
  2. Open Character Management (right-most icon).
  3. Click the import icon and choose the file, or drag the file onto the tavern window.
  4. Done. The card appears in your list. Click it to open a chat.

You can import several files at once. Chats, lorebooks and presets have their own import buttons in their own panels.

Imported cards are encrypted in your browser before they sync, like everything else. The server never sees the card or its picture.

Creating your own

Click in the character panel. You get an editor with the fields below. You don't need all of them: name, description and first message are enough to start.

Every field, explained

NameShown on messages and used as {{char}}.Keep it short and unique.
DescriptionThe core of the card: personality, appearance, backstory, speech style. Sent with every request.Be concrete. "Speaks in clipped sentences" beats "is blunt".
First messageThe greeting that begins each new chat. Also teaches the AI the writing style and length you want.The AI imitates it: write the way you want replies to look.

Macros you can use in any field

These placeholders are replaced when the prompt is built. They make cards reusable.

{{char}}   → the character's name
{{user}}   → your persona's name
{{random:red,green,blue}}   → one of the options
{{time}}  {{date}}  {{weekday}}   → current time info

Example: {{char}} squints at {{user}} and says nothing. becomes Captain Mira squints at Alex and says nothing. More in Slash commands & macros.

Tokens: the hidden cost of a card

Description, personality, scenario and examples are sent with every message, so they permanently use part of the context window. The editor shows a token count. As a rule of thumb, aim for a few hundred to ~1,500 tokens. Anything longer leaves less room for chat memory (see the context visualizer).

Managing your library

  • Search & sort by name, date or recent chats. Use tags to filter.
  • Favorite (star) characters to pin them to the top.
  • Duplicate a card before big experiments.
  • Export a card as PNG or JSON to share or back it up.
  • Delete removes the card. Its chats can be kept or deleted when you confirm.

Deleting is permanent: there is no server-side trash or backup you can ask the admin to restore, because the server can't read your data. Export what matters (also see backups).
