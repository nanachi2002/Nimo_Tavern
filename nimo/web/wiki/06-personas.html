The character card says who they are. A persona says who you are. The AI reads your persona's description every time, so it knows your name, appearance, role in the story, and anything it should treat as fact about you.

Creating a persona

  1. Open Persona Management (the smiley icon).
  2. Click Create and choose an avatar.
  3. Set the name that appears on your messages. This is what {{user}} turns into.
  4. Write the description: who you are in the story. Short is fine: "Alex, a 24-year-old courier carrying a stolen map. Nervous, quick on their feet."

Where the description goes

Under the description box there is a position setting. The default (in the main prompt, next to the character card) is right for nearly everyone. Other options insert the persona higher or lower in the chat, or into the Author's Note. If the AI keeps forgetting who you are, try a position closer to the end of the chat.

Several personas

You can keep as many personas as you want: "Alex the courier", "Lady Isolde", "narrator". Click a persona to activate it, and use the lock buttons:

  • Lock to this chat: this persona is used in this conversation only.
  • Lock to this character: whenever you open this character, this persona is picked automatically.
  • Default persona: used everywhere that isn't locked.
Don't over-write it

A persona is sent with every message. Keep it to what matters for the story, a few lines. Details about the world belong in a lorebook instead.

Your name and avatar are part of your encrypted data like everything else; they don't leave your browser except in the prompts you send to your AI provider.
