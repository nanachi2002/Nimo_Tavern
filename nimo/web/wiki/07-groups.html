A group chat lets two or more characters share one scene with you (and with each other). It's perfect for a party of adventurers, a tavern full of regulars, or a debate between two personalities.

Creating a group

  1. Open Character Management and click the Create group button.
  2. Name the group and add members from your character list.
  3. Choose how they take turns (activation strategy, below) and how their cards are combined.
  4. Click the group in the list to start chatting.

Activation strategies: who speaks?

StrategyBehaviorUse it for
Natural orderCharacters are picked by being mentioned by name, by talkativeness, then at random. Feels the most like a real conversation.Most scenes
List orderEveryone answers in the order of the member list, one after another.Predictable round-robin
ManualNobody speaks until you click a character's portrait to trigger them.Full control, saving tokens
Pooled orderEveryone speaks once before anyone speaks twice.Making sure all voices get heard

Each member also has a talkativeness slider (higher = speaks more often in natural order), and you can mute a member temporarily without removing them. Auto-mode makes the group keep talking by itself after a delay you choose.

Joining the cards

The generation mode decides how character cards enter the prompt:

  • Swap character cards (default): only the speaking character's card is sent, so each stays distinct and cheaper to run.
  • Join character cards (include muted / exclude muted): all cards are combined into one big description. Use it when the characters need to know each other's details, but it costs more tokens.
Keep groups cheap

A group with six verbose cards in Join mode can eat most of your context. Prefer Swap, trim the cards, and put shared facts in a group-wide lorebook.
