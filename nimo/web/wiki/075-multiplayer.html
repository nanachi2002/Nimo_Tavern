Multiplayer is a NimoTavern feature (it's not part of SillyTavern). Up to 6 players, each with their own persona, share one scene with an AI character and take turns, like a tabletop RPG. The host sets up the world, the lorebooks and the AI. Everyone else just joins and plays, live or at their own pace.

Multiplayer is not end-to-end encrypted

Your solo chats never leave your browser unencrypted. Multiplayer can't work that way: to run the AI for the whole group, even when the host is offline, the server reads the session while it writes the prompt. Sessions are encrypted at rest, never logged, and deleted when the host ends them. All the details.

Starting a session (host)

  1. Open a chat with the character you want as the AI (any character, and your existing messages can come along).
  2. Open the chat options menu (left of the message box) and choose Make multiplayer…
  3. Read the privacy note and tick the box.
  4. Set up the AI for the session. This is a separate connection from your solo one: pick a provider (OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Claude, Gemini or any OpenAI-compatible proxy), a model and an API key you only use for multiplayer. Click Test connection.
  5. Choose the rules (turn mode, number of players, an optional turn timer), write the world setting and tick the lorebooks the AI should use.
  6. Invite friends by their NimoTavern username and click Start multiplayer.

Joining (players)

When someone invites you, a small badge appears on the User Settings button and on Account. Open Account → Mailbox, read the privacy note and Accept. When you open your tavern, it asks which persona you want to play: the others see that persona's name, picture and description. The session then shows up in your character list as a chat with the AI character.

You can Decline, or Block someone so they can't invite you again. In Invite settings you can turn invites off completely.

Playing

A players bar sits above the chat: the AI and every player, a glow around whoever's turn it is, a for players who already played this round, and a green ring around people who are online right now. When it's your turn, the bar says Your turn! and you write your action in the normal message box.

Turn modes

ModeHow it goesGood for
Ordered rounds (default)AI → player 1 → player 2 → … → AI. Everyone plays once, in order, then the AI answers the whole round.Classic tabletop feel, scenes where order matters
Free roundsEveryone plays once per round, in any order. The AI answers once all players have posted (or when the host presses AI now).Groups that aren't all online at once
Strict alternationPlayer 1 → AI → player 2 → AI → … The AI answers after every player.Small groups, fast back-and-forth

The host can also set a turn timer (5 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours) after which a silent player is skipped automatically. It's off by default, so the game can wait for everyone.

Host controls

The host gets a Session button in the players bar:

  • Players: invite more people, change the turn order, remove a player.
  • Turns: AI now (make the AI answer right away), Skip the current player, Pause / Resume, Reroll the last AI reply, Stop a reply that's going wrong.
  • Messages: edit or delete any message with SillyTavern's usual pencil.
  • Settings: change the rules, the AI connection, the world setting, the lorebooks and the prompt at any time.
  • End the session: deletes everything stored on the server for it, for everyone. Each player keeps their own copy of the chat.

Players can pass their turn, edit or delete their own last message until the AI answers, and leave the session from the Session button.

What players see (and don't)

  • Players see: the chat, the AI character's name and picture, and everyone's persona name, picture and description.
  • Only the AI sees: the character definition, the world setting, the lorebooks and the prompt. Players can't read spoilers.
  • Nobody but the server sees: the host's multiplayer API key. It's stored encrypted and used only to run that session.
A key just for multiplayer

The multiplayer key is used by the server for everyone's turns. Create a separate key with your provider, give it a spending limit, and keep your main key for solo play (where it never leaves your browser).

Good to know

  • Multiplayer works with one AI character per session. For several AI characters in one scene, use a group chat (solo).
  • The AI uses a simplified version of SillyTavern's prompt: main prompt, character card, world setting, lorebook entries that match the recent messages, everyone's personas, example messages, recent history and a final instruction. Solo-only features like extensions, Author's Note and presets don't apply to it.
  • Sessions with no activity for 90 days are deleted from the server.
