RPG mode is a NimoTavern feature (it's not part of SillyTavern). It turns a chat into a game: after every reply the AI offers four choices, and the ones that can fail are settled with your character's stats and a dice roll. Health, money and inventory are tracked for you. Think Baldur's Gate or Cyberpunk 2077 dialogue, with any character card as the narrator.

RPG mode runs entirely in your browser. Your character sheet, rolls and adventures are stored in your normal encrypted chats and settings. The server never sees them.

The game loop

Every turn works the same way. The AI never rolls dice and never decides how a check ends: the app does that, so nobody can fudge a roll.

1The AI narratesand offers 4 choices
2You pick oneor write your own action
3The app rollsd10 + your attribute vs difficulty
4The story reactsXP, health, loot, new choices

A separate game mode

  • RPG adventures are chats marked as games. Once a chat is an RPG adventure, it can't go back to a normal chat.
  • RPG characters are personas made with the RPG character maker. They only show up and can only be played in RPG adventures, and your normal personas stay in normal chats.
  • Each adventure is played by one RPG character, like a save file. It keeps its own health, money and inventory, so the same character can live several adventures at once.

Quick start

  1. Make an RPG character. In the persona tab tap Create and choose RPG character (or RPG version of your current persona). Spend your points in the skill web.
  2. Open a chat with the character you want as the narrator.
  3. Open the chat options menu (left of the message box) and choose New RPG adventure. ( Turn chat into RPG… converts the chat you're in instead, for good.)
  4. Pick a world and your RPG character, then tap Begin the adventure above the message box to get your first choices.
