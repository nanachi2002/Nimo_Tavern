An adventure needs two things: a world, which sets the tone, and an RPG character, which is you.

Choosing a world

You pick the world when you start an adventure. It decides the tone the narrator uses, what the six attributes are called, the money and the starting kit, and gives the interface its own look.

  • Fantasy has a tabletop feel: gold trim and serif headings.

The narrator reads the character card and the chat, works out the universe (a superhero city, a spaceship, a haunted house, a Marvel-style world...) and keeps it consistent, with neutral attribute names so they fit anything. Pick it when the card you're chatting with isn't a sword-or-chrome story.

Making a character

  1. In the persona tab tap Create. You are asked: Normal persona, RPG character, or RPG version of your current persona (it copies the name, description and picture and leaves the original alone).
  2. Give them a name, a picture, a class and a short description. The AI reads it.
  3. Build the character in the skill web (below). Every attribute starts at 2 and you have 24 skill points.
  4. Any points you don't spend are kept and can be used later, in the adventure.

The skill web

Attributes and perks are one system, like in Cyberpunk 2077: there are no separate steppers. Each of the six attributes has its own web. The trunk in the middle is the attribute itself (rank 3 up to 10, one point each) and perks branch off both sides. Watch a web being built:

Strength webPoints left: 2423222120191817161514131210
Green: attribute ranks (the trunk). Blue: perks on the branches. Gold: the capstone.
  • A perk needs its attribute at a minimum rank (3, 5, 7 or 9 on the main chains, 4, 6 or 8 on the side chains) and the perks it is linked to.
  • The capstone at the top of each web needs rank 10 and the end of either side, and costs 2 points. That's 15 perks per attribute, 90 in all.
  • Tap a node to read it. Unlock spends the points; Refund takes a choice back (only if nothing else depends on it).
  • Health is 10 + 2 × Strength, plus anything perks add.

🃏 Cheat mode

Turn it on in the character maker for unlimited skill points, now and in every adventure. The web's paths and ranks still apply, so you build the character you want branch by branch, all the way to the capstones. The character shows a 🃏 badge.

Characters stay in their lane

RPG characters only appear in RPG adventures and normal personas only in normal chats. From the persona tab, 🎲 RPG lists your RPG characters: tap one to see its build and web.
