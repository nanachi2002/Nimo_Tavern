Some cards are different: No roll needed choices always work, and locked choices show the attribute you're missing (some actions need, say, Strength 8 just to try). On a computer, press 1 to 4 to pick a card (not while you're typing).

The roll

A check is your attribute + 1d10 against the difficulty. Tap a card and a die rolls on screen: die + attribute + perk bonuses = total, and the verdict is stamped on.

A natural 10 is a critical success: roll again and add it, so a total can go well past 15. A natural 1 is a fumble: roll again and subtract it. Perks can make criticals easier (a 9 or even an 8 counts) or remove fumbles.

Even a weak attribute keeps a small chance (a natural 10 explodes), and even a legend can fail (a natural 1). Pick your fights, or specialize.

Failing is part of the game

  • Failing never ends the story. It costs something: health, money, time or a new complication.
  • At 0 health you're down: captured, rescued or waking up hurt, never dead.
  • The app rolls, not the AI. The AI suggests choices, difficulties and what you gain or lose. NimoTavern checks and limits all of it, so the AI can't fudge a roll or invent impossible numbers.
  • Swiping keeps your roll. A new swipe rewrites how the result is told, not the result. Health, money and items always follow the replies you keep, so deleting or swiping a message rolls its effects back too.

Free actions

Prefer to improvise? Write your own action in the message box as usual. If the outcome is uncertain, the AI asks for a roll and a single roll card appears, so even free actions follow the dice.
