Characters grow as you play. Experience (XP) turns into levels, levels turn into skill points, and skill points turn into a stronger, more specialised character.

Rolls and achievements fill the bar. A level gives skill points to spend in the web.
  • Every roll gives XP: Easy 5, Standard 10, Hard 20, Very hard 35 (×1.5 on a critical), and 3 for a failure, because you learn from mistakes.
  • The narrator awards XP for achievements, from a fixed scale it is taught: trivial 0–5, minor 10–25, moderate 30–60, major 80–150, epic 200–400. NimoTavern caps every reward to its category, so the AI can't hand out a fortune.
  • Each level gives skill points: 1 per level, and 1 more every second level. The max level is 50.
  • Level up gets its own screen with the points you earned and a button straight into the skill web.

Spending points

The badge next to your name shows your level and XP, and glows with a number when you have skill points to spend. Tap it to open the web and keep specializing.

  • What you pick when making the character is permanent in that adventure; what you buy while playing can be refunded.
  • Progress belongs to each adventure, so the same character can start fresh in a new one.

The bar and the character sheet

  • The bar above the message box shows your portrait with a level ring, health, experience and money, plus buttons for the skill web and the character sheet. The chevron hides the choices to give the chat more room.
  • The sheet shows a radar chart of your attributes, your perks, your inventory and your recent rolls.
