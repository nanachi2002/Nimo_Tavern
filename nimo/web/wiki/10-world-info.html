A lorebook (World Info) is a collection of small notes about your world: places, factions, items, rules, secrets. Each note has trigger keywords. When a keyword appears in the recent chat, the note is injected into the prompt, so the AI "remembers" it, and when it doesn't appear, the note costs nothing.

Try the trigger logic

This is exactly the idea. Edit the message and watch which entries wake up and what gets added to the prompt.

Creating a lorebook

  1. Open World Info () and click New World. Name it.
  2. Add an entry with New Entry.
  3. Fill in the keys (comma-separated triggers) and the content: the note itself, written as plain facts.
  4. Make it active: select it as a global book, or link it to a character or chat (below).

Entry settings that matter

SettingMeaning
KeysWords or phrases that trigger the entry (substring match by default; enable whole words to avoid "art" matching "start"). Regex keys are supported.
Optional filter / secondary keysAn extra condition: the entry fires only if the primary key and any/all of the secondary keys appear (AND ANY / AND ALL), or none (NOT).
Constant Always included, no keyword needed. Use sparingly: it's the same as putting text in the card.
PositionWhere it's inserted: before/after the character card, at the top/bottom of the Author's Note, or at a chosen depth in the chat.
OrderWhen the budget is tight, higher order wins priority.
ProbabilityChance (%) that a triggered entry actually fires. Good for random events.
Inclusion groupsOnly one entry in a group may fire at a time (like rumors).
Sticky / cooldown / delayKeep an entry active for N messages once triggered, silence it for N messages, or wait N messages before it can fire.
RecursionLets an entry's text trigger other entries. Powerful for linked lore, also easy to overdo.

Global, character and chat lorebooks

  • Global: active in every chat. Use for universal world rules.
  • Character: attached to one character (in the character editor). Active whenever you chat with them.
  • Chat: attached to a single conversation, via the chat's options menu.
  • Persona: attached to your persona, for facts about you.

Global settings (top of the panel)

SettingMeaning
Scan depthHow many recent messages are searched for keywords. Higher finds more, but may trigger things from old messages.
Budget (% of context)The most of your context lorebooks may use. Entries beyond it are skipped by order.
Min activationsKeep scanning deeper until at least this many entries fire.
Recursive scanningEnables entries to trigger entries.
Writing good entries
  • One topic per entry, written as facts ("Ashfang is…"), not instructions.
  • Include the name, alternate names and typos as keys.
  • Keep entries short (50–150 tokens). Ten small entries beat one giant one.
