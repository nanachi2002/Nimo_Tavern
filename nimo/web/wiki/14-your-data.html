Where your data lives

In a normal SillyTavern, your data is a folder on a computer. In NimoTavern it lives in two places, both encrypted:

  1. Your browser keeps an encrypted local copy (so the tavern opens in under a second and works with a flaky connection). It's unlocked with keys that only exist on your device.
  2. The server keeps an encrypted copy so your data follows you to other devices. It holds unreadable blobs; it has no key to open them.

When you save a character, send a message or change a setting, your browser encrypts the changed files and uploads them in the background. The little indicator in the corner shows pending uploads.

Using several devices

  1. Log in on the new device with the same username and password.
  2. Wait for the first download. The encrypted data comes down and is unlocked locally. This takes seconds for small accounts, longer for big ones (hundreds of MB of chats and images).
  3. Keep using it. Changes sync both ways. After the first load, the tavern starts from the local cache and only fetches what changed.
Two devices at the same time

Sync is per file and "last write wins". If you edit the same chat on two devices at once, one version will overwrite the other. Finish on one device, wait for the upload indicator to clear, then switch.

Before closing the tab

If uploads are still pending, your browser warns you before you leave. Let it finish: until then the newest changes exist only on that device.

Storage space

Each account has a storage quota (1 GB by default), shown with a bar at the top of your Account page. Text compresses well, so chats are small; images (character PNGs, backgrounds, avatars) take most of the space. Free space by deleting unused characters and chats, or large uploaded backgrounds.

Backups and leaving

You are never locked in. Under Account → Your data → Export decrypted backup (.zip) your browser decrypts everything and builds a zip using SillyTavern's own folder layout (characters, chats, worlds, settings, personas…). You can unzip it into a local SillyTavern's data/default-user folder.

The backup is NOT encrypted

It contains your chats and your API keys in plain form. Store it somewhere safe (an encrypted drive or password manager vault) and delete copies you no longer need.

Importing things in

  • Characters, chats, lorebooks, presets, themes, personas: use the import buttons in each SillyTavern panel; they work exactly as in normal SillyTavern.
  • Whole-account zip import: not available yet. Individual imports are the way for now.
  • Moved from the old SillyTavern server? Your account was prepared in advance: log in with your old username and password and your data is encrypted and imported automatically, see Troubleshooting.

Logging out

Logging out wipes the local copy and the keys from that device. Your data is safe on the server (encrypted), and logging in again re-downloads it. Always log out on shared or public computers. If you can't (lost phone, forgotten session), use Log out everywhere on the Account page to end all sessions at once.

Changing to a different account on the same browser also wipes the previous user's local data first.

What happens if…

SituationResult
You clear your browser's site data while logged inLocal copy and keys vanish. Log in again; nothing is lost on the server.
You go offlineYou keep chatting if your AI provider is reachable (or a local model). Changes upload when you're back online.
The server goes downYour local copy keeps working; sync resumes later.
You forget your password but have the recovery phraseYou can set a new password with it, see here.
You lose both password and recovery phraseThe data cannot be recovered by anyone. That's the cost of true privacy.
