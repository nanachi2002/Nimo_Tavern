Open your account page with User Settings → Account in the tavern (or go to /account). Here's everything on it, and the two situations where it matters most.

Your recovery phrase

When you signed up, you were shown 12 words. They're a second key to your data. Because nobody else can reset your password, the phrase is the only way back in if you forget it.

rivercandleorbitvelvetharbormapleglimmertokendriftamberforestwhisper

(An example. Yours is different and only shown once.)

Store it like a master key
  • Save it in a password manager or write it on paper kept somewhere safe.
  • Never share it, and never type it into any site other than NimoTavern's own /recover page. Anyone with your username and phrase can take over your account.
  • It's shown only at sign-up and when you generate a new one. NimoTavern can't show it again because it doesn't know it.

Forgot your password?

Recover your account
 Reset password
Done! Now save your NEW recovery phrase: the old one no longer works.
  1. Open /recover (link on the login page).
  2. Enter your username and the 12 words (spelling and order matter).
  3. Choose a new password (at least 10 characters).
  4. Save your NEW recovery phrase. The old one is retired the moment the reset completes.

Your chats and characters are intact: the recovery re-locks the same data key, it doesn't re-create it.

Choosing a good password

  • Length beats complexity. Four or five unrelated words ("copper-lantern-orbit-pancake-ferry") is strong and memorable.
  • Use one you've never used elsewhere. A reused password that leaks somewhere else lets attackers try it here.
  • Use a password manager and store the recovery phrase in it too (or on paper, separately).

Deleting your account

  1. Export a backup first if you might want your data later.
  2. Open Account → Delete account and confirm with your password.
  3. Done. All your encrypted blobs, sessions and account records are removed from the database, and your browser's local copy is wiped. The username becomes free again.

This cannot be undone and cannot be recovered from backups: there are no plaintext backups, and the encrypted copies are deleted with the account.

Signing in from a new place

Your password is all you need on a new device. The recovery phrase is not a login method for daily use, only for resets. If a browser is unsupported (in-app browsers inside Discord, Instagram and similar), you'll see an instruction to open the page in Safari or Chrome instead.
