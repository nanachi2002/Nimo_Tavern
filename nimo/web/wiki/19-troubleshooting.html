Login & browser problems

"This browser can't run NimoTavern" / the login button is disabled

You're probably in an in-app browser (opened from Discord, Instagram, Messenger, TikTok…) or a private tab. Those block the "Service Worker" technology NimoTavern runs on.

  1. Tap ••• or the share icon and choose Open in Safari / Open in Chrome.
  2. Leave private/incognito mode.
  3. Log in again with the same details.
"Couldn't get CSRF token. Please refresh the page."

The tavern's local engine wasn't ready when the page started (common on phones when the connection is slow or the browser just woke up). NimoTavern now retries by itself once. If you still see an error screen: tap Try again, then Log in again. If it persists, make sure you're in Safari/Chrome (not an app's browser) and not in a private tab, and send a screenshot of the message when you ask for help.

"Wrong username or password"

Check caps lock and keyboard autocorrect (phones love capitalizing the first letter of the username). Usernames aren't case-sensitive but the password is. If you really forgot it, use the recovery page with your 12 words.

I got logged out by itself

Sessions last 30 days, and clearing your browser's site data, using "Log out everywhere", or logging into another account on the same browser removes the local keys. Log in again; your data is still on the server.

The page stays on a loading logo or a blank screen
  • Reload once (Ctrl+Shift+R for a hard refresh).
  • Disable extensions that modify pages (ad blockers with aggressive rules, dark-mode extensions) for this site.
  • In developer tools → Application → Service Workers, click Unregister, reload, and log in again.

Migrating from the old SillyTavern server

How do I get my old chats and characters?

Accounts from the previous SillyTavern server were prepared in advance. Just go to the login page and enter your old username and password. NimoTavern recognizes the account, creates your encrypted account with the same password, imports everything (with a progress counter), shows you a new recovery phrase to save, and deletes the old unencrypted copy from the server when it's done. Keep the tab open until it finishes.

The import stopped halfway

Nothing is lost. Log in again with the same password and the import resumes. Use Safari/Chrome, keep the screen on, and stay on a stable connection.

My account says it's already taken or my password doesn't work

Use exactly your old username (letters, numbers and _ . - only) and the password you used on the old server. Accounts whose names weren't allowed in NimoTavern were renamed; if you're unsure, ask the person who invited you.

Connections & replies

"Failed to fetch" or a CORS error when generating

The provider (or your local server) blocks requests from web pages. For hosted providers, turn on the relay in Account (fallback mode). For a local server enable CORS or allowed-origins in its settings. See connection troubleshooting.

The AI forgets earlier events

The chat outgrew the context window. See Prompt & context for fixes: raise context size, trim cards, use Summarize or lorebooks.

Replies are repetitive or too short/long

Adjust response length, temperature, and repetition settings, see AI settings & presets. Also edit repeated phrases out of recent messages: the AI copies what it sees.

My local model works on my PC but not on my phone

localhost on a phone is the phone itself. Give the model server an HTTPS address reachable from the phone and use that URL.

Sync & storage

My new device is slow the first time

It's downloading and decrypting your whole encrypted tavern once. Big accounts (hundreds of MB) take a minute or so on a good connection. After that it opens from the local copy almost instantly.

A change didn't appear on my other device

Wait for the upload indicator on the first device to clear, then reload the second one. Avoid editing the same chat on two devices at once, see Your data.

"Storage full" or uploads failing

You've reached your quota (see the bar on the Account page). Delete large unused images or old chats. Backgrounds and character PNGs use the most space.

A feature is missing (vectors, captioning, installing extensions)

Features that need server-side AI models or server-side installation are disabled to keep your data unreadable by the server. See the extensions table.

Privacy FAQ

Can the admin read my chats?

No. They have ciphertext they can't open, with no key. Here's how, and how to check.

Can the admin reset my password?

No, and that's by design. Only your recovery phrase can.

Do you see my API key?

No. It's stored encrypted and sent only from your browser to your provider. (If you opt into the relay for a request, it passes through server memory for that request only.)

Is my data used to train anything?

NimoTavern can't read it, so it can't be. Your AI provider has its own data policy for the prompts you send them, so read it.

Is this official SillyTavern?

No. It's an independent privacy-focused fork, built on SillyTavern's code with full credit to its contributors, and licensed under the same AGPL-3.0. It isn't affiliated with the SillyTavern team, so please don't ask them for NimoTavern support.

Still stuck?

Ask whoever invited you, or open an issue on the source repository. Never include your password, recovery phrase or API key in a bug report.
